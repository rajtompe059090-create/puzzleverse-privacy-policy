PuzzleVerse Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

PuzzleVerse ("we", "our", or "the app") is a puzzle game application developed to provide users with an enjoyable collection of casual puzzle games.

1. Information We Collect

PuzzleVerse is designed to provide gameplay features primarily on the user's device. We do not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, address, phone number, or password to play the game.

2. Advertising

PuzzleVerse uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and process certain device and advertising information, such as advertising identifiers, approximate location, app interaction information, and diagnostic information, according to Google's policies and the user's device settings.

For more information about how Google handles data for advertising, please review Google's privacy information: Google Privacy Policy .

3. Local Data

PuzzleVerse may store gameplay progress, scores, settings, and other game-related information locally on the user's device. This information is used to provide the game's features and improve the gameplay experience.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. Advertising and related services may process information as described in the relevant service providers' privacy policies.

5. Children's Privacy

PuzzleVerse is not specifically directed at children under the applicable minimum age. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the app. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Third-Party Services

PuzzleVerse may use third-party services such as Google AdMob for advertising. These services may collect information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or PuzzleVerse, please contact the app developer through the contact information provided on the Google Play Store listing.

10. Consent

By using PuzzleVerse, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy and agree to the practices described above.
